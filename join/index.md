## Join the MIC Lab

MIC Lab welcomes students and researchers interested in developing rigorous
control and learning methods for real-world mobility systems.
Current opportunities and inquiry details are listed below.

### Graduate Research Opportunities (M.S. / Ph.D.)

We welcome students interested in applying control theory, optimization, and
learning to mobility systems. A background in control systems or prior research
experience is helpful but not required; strong mathematical reasoning and
problem-solving skills provide a solid foundation for research in our group.

To inquire, please email [Prof. Kyunghwan Choi]({{ site.url }}/members/khChoi/)
at <a href="mailto:kh.choi@kaist.ac.kr">kh.choi@kaist.ac.kr</a> with your CV
and academic transcript.

### Undergraduate Research Internships

We welcome undergraduate students interested in gaining research experience
through projects in the following areas:

- Optimal control of electric drives
- Motion planning and control for mobile robots, including agricultural
  robots, unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) for defense applications, and
  coordinated delivery-robot fleets
- Learning-based whole-body control of humanoid robots for logistics tasks

To inquire, please email [Prof. Kyunghwan Choi]({{ site.url }}/members/khChoi/)
at <a href="mailto:kh.choi@kaist.ac.kr">kh.choi@kaist.ac.kr</a> with a brief
introduction, your CV, and the topic(s) that interest you.


### Postdoctoral Positions

We welcome postdoctoral applications on a rolling basis. Current areas of
particular interest include:

- Online learning-based optimal control for mobility systems
- Optimal control of electric drives, including predictive torque control and online model learning

To inquire, please email [Prof. Kyunghwan Choi]({{ site.url }}/members/khChoi/)
at <a href="mailto:kh.choi@kaist.ac.kr">kh.choi@kaist.ac.kr</a> with your CV
and a brief description of your research interests.
