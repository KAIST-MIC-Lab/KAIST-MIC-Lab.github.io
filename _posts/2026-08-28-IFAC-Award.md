Ph.D. student **Seunghun Jang**, received the **Silver Award** in **the Benchmark Problems: Thermal Management Strategy Design for Electric Vehicles under Connectivity**, at the **2026 International Federation of Automatic Control (IFAC)** conference held at *BEXCO* in **Busan, South Korea** from **August 24 to 28, 2026**.

Congratulations to **Seunghun Jang** for his outstanding achievements!
