We participated in the **<a href="https://www.ifac2026.org/fairDash.do">2026 International Federation of Automatic Control (IFAC)</a>** conference held at *BEXCO* in **Busan, South Korea** from **August 24 to 28, 2026**.

From left to right:
- <a href="{{site.url}}/members/nsErega/">**Naol Samuel Erega**</a> (M.S. Student)
- <a href="{{site.url}}/members/jyKim/">**Jiyun Kim**</a> (Researcher)
  - Presented <a href="{{site.url}}/publications/2026-CARE-Planner/">**CARE Planner : Constrained Attention and Risk-aware Planning for Imitation based Autonomous Driving**</a>
- <a href="{{site.url}}/members/msRyu/">**Myeongseok Ryu**</a> (Ph.D. Student)

- <a href="{{site.url}}/members/shJang/">**Seunghun Jang**</a> (Ph.D. Student)

- <a href="{{site.url}}/members/hcLee/">**Hyochan Lee**</a> (Ph.D. Student)
  - Presented <a href="{{site.url}}/publications/2026-online-constrained/">**Online Constrained Reinforcement Learning for Optimal Tracking**</a>

We presented our papers at the congress, respectively, and had a great time meeting many researchers in the field of automatic control. 
We hope to meet again in the near future!
